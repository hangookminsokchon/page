I am broadly interested in developing mathematically sound computational frameworks/methods for analyzing complex structures in biomedical data, including high-resolution pathology images and single-cell omics. I am particularly interested in spatial statistics, topological data analysis (TDA), and statistically robust machine learning approaches for modeling biologically meaningful problems.

I am a rising senior at the University of North Carolina at Chapel Hill, pursuing a double major in Mathematics and Statistics & Analytics. I plan to pursue a PhD in Mathematics or Statistics beginning in Fall 2027, and I welcome opportunities to collaborate with research groups in spatial statistics, topological data analysis, or computational biology.
